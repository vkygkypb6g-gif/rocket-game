    How to play

    This is fake money — you start with $1,000. A launch-angle needle sweeps above the pad. Tap the sky, press Space, or hit Launch to lock the angle and fire. The rocket flies a ballistic arc; the further it goes, the higher the multiplier. The first stretch of ground is a short-flight zone that pays under 0.5x. Hidden meteors are scattered in a fresh layout every round and only appear as they come into view. Hit one and you bounce with a boost — chains can send you a long way. Wind shifts each launch. No angle is a sure thing.
